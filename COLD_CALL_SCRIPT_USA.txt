SECTION 1: THE OPENER (FIRST 10 SECONDS)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[RING... they pick up]

YOU:

you at a bad time?"

If they say "Kind of, what is this about?" → Say:

  "Totally, I'll be super quick. We're a performance marketing agency and I was

   run by you. It'll take literally 60 seconds, and if it's not relevant I'll
   let you go. Fair enough?"

SECTION 2: THE HOOK (PERMISSION + PATTERN INTERRUPT)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

"So the reason I'm reaching out is — we work with e-commerce and D2C brands in
the US to scale their paid ads on Meta and Google. The average ROAS we deliver
is 4.2x, and we've helped brands cut their customer acquisition cost by up to 48%
within the first 90 days.

I was looking at [Company Name] and — [INSERT ONE SPECIFIC OBSERVATION]:

  → For E-comm:   "I noticed you're running ads but your product page doesn't
                   have a dedicated landing page — that alone could be costing you
                   20 to 30 percent of conversions."

  → For Social:   "I saw your Instagram — great product, but your content cadence
                   and ad creatives look like they could be a lot more aggressive.
                   We took one brand from 12,000 to 280,000 followers in 6 months

                   That's actually a big opportunity — your competitors are, and
                   we could get you in front of a qualified audience fast."

The reason I'm calling specifically is I think there's a real opportunity here
and I didn't want to just send a cold email. Does that kind of result resonate
with what you're working on right now?"

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3: THE DISCOVERY (ASK QUESTIONS — DON'T PITCH YET)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[Once they're engaged, slow down. Ask these in a natural conversation — not all
at once. Pick 2-3 based on the flow.]

DISCOVERY QUESTIONS:

1. "What does your current marketing setup look like — are you running paid ads
   in-house, with an agency, or not at all right now?"

2. "What's the biggest frustration you have with your marketing right now —
   is it lead quality, cost per acquisition, content, or something else?"

3. "What does a successful month look like for you — revenue-wise or in terms
   of new customers?"

4. "Have you worked with a marketing agency before? How did that go?"

5. "What's your current monthly ad spend budget, roughly — just so I know if
   what we do makes sense for where you're at?"

[LISTEN. Take notes. Mirror their language in your pitch.]

         Handle Every Objection with Confidence and Empathy

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REBUTTAL 1: "We already have an agency."
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

"That's great — most of the brands we end up working with actually came from
another agency. I'm not here to bash anyone.

Can I ask — are you happy with the results you're getting right now? Because
a lot of our clients switched not because their agency was bad, but because
they weren't growing at the pace they expected.

We work alongside existing setups all the time, or we do a free audit first
just to give you a second opinion. Would that be useful?"

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REBUTTAL 2: "We handle marketing in-house."
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

"Totally understand — a lot of our best clients started that way. In-house
teams are great for brand knowledge and speed.

The question is always capacity and specialization. What we bring is a
dedicated performance team with 10+ years of combined experience, full
creative production, and daily optimization — without the cost of expanding
your headcount.

We're not here to replace your team. A lot of times we work alongside internal
teams and just handle the paid acquisition and creative side. Would it make
sense to just compare notes and see where the gaps are?"

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

"I hear you — I appreciate the honesty.

Can I just ask one quick question before I let you go? What would need to be

past experience with agencies, or something else?

[Listen to their answer]

Got it — and that makes total sense. I'll reach out again in [X months] when
the timing is better. Is it okay if I send you one email with a case study

"Understood — we typically start working at a minimum ad spend of $1,500/month.

CLOSING NOTES FOR REPS

ALWAYS REMEMBER:
─────────────────
1. Your GOAL on a cold call is NOT to sell — it's to book the meeting.
2. Ask more than you talk. Whoever asks questions controls the conversation.
3. Mirror their language. If they say "struggling with leads" — use that word.
4. Never argue. Validate, pivot, and re-engage.
5. Silence is your friend. After asking a question — shut up and listen.
6. Rejection is data. If someone hangs up, ask yourself: what would I do
   differently next time?
